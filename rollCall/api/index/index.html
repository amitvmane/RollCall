RollCall

Attendance for your group, made simple. Open a rollcall, let everyone vote in, out or maybe, and track who shows up — in Telegram or right here on the web.

➕ Add to a Telegram group 🌐 Start on the web — no Telegram

Already in a group? Open the link your organiser shared, or see your groups.

What you get

🗳️

One-tap voting

Members reply /in, /out or /maybe — or tap the panel buttons.

📊

Stats & streaks

Per-member attendance rates, streaks and personal bests, tracked automatically.

🌐

Vote from the web

A permanent web link lets people vote even when Telegram is down — admins can manage the whole group there too.

⏰

Reminders & templates

Auto-close rollcalls, send reminders, and reuse recurring event templates.

Get started in 30 seconds

✈️ With Telegram

  1. Add @{{BOT_USERNAME}} to your group.
  2. An admin runs /src to start a rollcall.
  3. Everyone votes with /in, /out or /maybe — or taps the buttons.
  4. Run /erc to close it and record the stats.

🌐 Without Telegram

  1. Sign in with your email and create a group.
  2. Tap 🔗 Invite people and share the link anywhere.
  3. People sign in by email and join; you approve them.
  4. Start a rollcall — everyone votes on the page and gets an email or a notification.

Mixed group? People without Telegram can join a Telegram group's web page by email and vote as themselves.
